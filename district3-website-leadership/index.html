Welcome to District 3

There is strength
in fellowship.

✧

A thriving ministry needs more than a place to preach. It needs people to pray with, wisdom to draw from, and fellow laborers who believe in the same Gospel.

District 3 of the Texas State Council is part of the Pentecostal Churches of the Apostolic Faith International, Inc. (PCAFI). Our mission calls us to strengthen local churches, equip leadership, and cultivate unity across the body of Christ.

Whether you pastor an established congregation, are planting a new work, or simply desire to learn about our fellowship, we invite you to begin a conversation with district leadership.

OUR MISSION

Build the Kingdom.
Strengthen the people.

To serve as a unified fellowship of Apostolic churches, firmly rooted in biblical truth, committed to holiness, empowered by the Holy Ghost, and dedicated to advancing God's Kingdom throughout Texas.

Read the full mission statement +

We are committed to winning souls to Jesus Christ, equipping believers for spiritual growth and effective ministry, developing strong leadership, strengthening local churches, and cultivating an atmosphere where worship, praise, and spiritual excellence are demonstrated without limitation.

Through prayer, fellowship, biblical education, leadership development, and collaborative ministry, we endeavor to support the continued growth and advancement of the Texas State Council while strengthening and developing District 3 as a place of spiritual unity, accountability, and Kingdom purpose.

In alignment with the vision and leadership of our Presiding Bishop, Bishop Lambert W. Gates, Sr., we are committed to working together in faith, purpose, and excellence to fulfill the mandate of the Gospel, build stronger churches, develop effective leaders, and impact our communities for the glory of God.

“Unity, service, and spiritual excellence.”
OUR VISION

One fellowship.
Stronger churches.

To establish a strong, unified, and spiritually thriving Apostolic fellowship that advances the Kingdom of God through sound biblical teaching, empowered leadership, strengthened churches, intentional discipleship, and unwavering commitment to the Apostolic faith.

We envision District 3 as a model of unity, spiritual maturity, collaboration, and Kingdom advancement, contributing to the continued growth and success of the Texas State Council of the PCAFI.

Common Questions

A conversation
is the first step.

Here are some starting points as you prayerfully consider fellowship with District 3.

What kind of ministry can inquire about fellowship?+

Pastors, church planters, and ministers who are exploring Apostolic-Pentecostal fellowship are welcome to make an inquiry. District leadership can clarify any applicable eligibility requirements.

What is the affiliation process?+

Begin by introducing your ministry through the inquiry form. This is an expression of interest, not an application approval. District leadership will discuss current organizational requirements and any formal next steps.

Will my church retain its identity?+

Questions about church governance, affiliation, and ministerial credentials deserve a direct answer from authorized leadership. We encourage pastors to discuss all expectations before making any commitment.

What does District 3 emphasize?+

Our mission highlights sound Apostolic teaching, holiness, prayer, genuine fellowship, spiritual empowerment, leadership development, and strengthening local churches.

How can I learn more about the national PCAFI?+

You can visit the official PCAFI website for its history, leadership, ministries, and current organizational resources.
